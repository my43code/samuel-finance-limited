# Website content and discovery

Reviewed 15 November 2026 for information architecture and visitor questions:

- BSP PNG personal loans: https://www.bsp.com.pg/personal-banking/loans/personal-loan/
- Westpac PNG personal loans: https://www.westpac.com.pg/personal/personal-loans/personal-loans-secured-and-unsecured/
- Google Search Central, sitemaps: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview

The resulting guide text is original general education. Competitor rates,
products, eligibility rules, licenses, turnaround times and borrowing limits
are not presented as Samuel Finance information. Samuel Finance availability,
eligibility, pricing and terms must be confirmed by the company.

The homepage reads published CMS news and documents on each server request.
The sliders expose the latest nine updates and seven documents, alongside
permanent guide and preparation links. The full listing pages remain available.
Visitors can swipe, use navigation controls, or pause automatic movement.
Reduced-motion visitors start with automatic movement paused.

`/sitemap.xml` reads published public detail pages independently of homepage
limits. `/robots.txt` advertises it, and `/site-map` is a human-readable directory.
Admin/API/auth routes and drafts are excluded. The sitemap supports discovery;
search engines decide when and whether pages are indexed. A site owner can submit
`https://samuelfinancelimited.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console after
verifying domain ownership. No Search Console credentials are stored here.
